About & credits

A live 3D view of the Galileo navigation satellites and ESA’s Celeste LEO-PNT demonstrators. Orbits are computed in your browser from the latest published orbital elements.

Data

  • Orbital elements (OMM): from CelesTrak, maintained by Dr. T.S. Kelso, using the current GP data for the “galileo” group and for objects named LPNTSAT. The underlying orbital data comes from the U.S. Space Force’s 18th Space Defense Squadron via Space-Track.org.
  • Galileo service status: from the European GNSS Service Centre (GSC).
  • Country outlines: world-atlas, derived from Natural Earth.

Satellite data is refreshed a few times a day and may lag behind reality. Being listed here does not mean a satellite is healthy or in service; use the official service status for that.

Built with

React, D3, satellite.js (SGP4 propagation) and Vite, each under its own open-source licence.

Fonts

Text is set in Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono, designed by the Braille Institute with Applied Design Works for maximum legibility. Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and served from this site, so no request goes to a third party.

Privacy

This site sets no cookies. It uses privacy-friendly analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) that count visits and page loads without tracking individuals, and it stores only your display choices (light or dark appearance, hiding the legend, showing or hiding the time controls, the 3D orbit trace) in your browser.
